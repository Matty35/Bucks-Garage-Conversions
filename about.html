One trade, done properly

We convert garages. That's it.

Not extensions on Mondays and driveways on Fridays. Every week, the same transformation: cold storage into warm living space, done to a standard building control signs off first time.

Why specialise

The case for a one-trade firm

A garage conversion is a small project with an unusual concentration of technical traps: a slab below house floor level with no membrane under it, single-skin walls that need lining without losing the room, fire separation rules that change with the layout, and — around Aylesbury especially — deeds and planning conditions that can stop a project before it starts.

A general builder meets these problems occasionally. We meet them every week, on the same estates, under the same building control officers. That repetition is the product: quotes that don't move, programmes that hold, and inspections that pass first visit.

How we work

The commitments behind every quote

Fixed written quotes

Itemised, with drawings and building control fees included. If the survey can't de-risk something, we say so in writing before you sign — not in week three.

Building control on every job

Every project is applied for, inspected and signed off. You get the completion certificate, the electrical certificates and the paperwork trail your future sale will need.

The paperwork checks first

Covenant, planning and party wall checks are part of the free survey. If your project has a legal snag, you find out before spending money, not after.

Honest about value

If converting your garage would hurt your resale — losing the only parking on a tight street — we'll tell you, and suggest the partial option or none at all.

Where we work

Aylesbury first, Buckinghamshire wide

We're based in the Aylesbury area and most of our work is within half an hour of it: Kingsbrook, Berryfields, Buckingham Park, Fairford Leys, Watermead, Wendover, Aston Clinton, Weston Turville, Tring and Thame. We take projects across Buckinghamshire where the diary allows.

Working close to home matters for a conversion: it's how we know which estates carry garage covenants, which streets have tight parking where losing a garage stings, and what the local building control teams want to see at each inspection.
