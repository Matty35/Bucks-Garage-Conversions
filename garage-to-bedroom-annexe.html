Garage to bedroom or annexe

Non-negotiables

What makes a legal bedroom

These are the specifics building control will measure on sign-off day. If a quote doesn't mention them, ask why.

  • Escape window: a clear openable area of at least 0.33m², no dimension under 450mm, and a cill no higher than 1100mm from the finished floor. We specify the window around this before we think about how it looks — then make it look right anyway.
  • Fire separation: where the bedroom adjoins the rest of the house through an integral garage layout, fire-rated construction and mains-linked smoke detection on the escape route.
  • Warmth for sleeping: full Part L insulation to floor, walls and ceiling — a bedroom gets used at the coldest hours of the day, so there's no hiding a poor thermal job.
  • Ventilation: background trickle ventilation plus an opening window; humidity-controlled extract if we're adding an en-suite.
  • Sound: acoustic insulation in the lining walls, because a bedroom next to a living room needs it in both directions.

The annexe caveat — honestly stated

A dependent relative's room that shares the house's entrance and services is usually fine under permitted development. A self-contained annexe — own kitchen, own entrance, capable of separate occupation — can be treated as creating a new dwelling, which needs planning permission and is regularly refused. Detached garages raise the bar further. We'll tell you at the survey which side of that line your plan sits on, and how to design within it.

FAQ

Bedroom & annexe questions

Can I market it as a bedroom when I sell?

Only if it has the building control completion certificate and meets the escape-window rules. A "bedroom" without them gets downgraded by the buyer's surveyor — often after it's already affected the offer. Ours are signed off as habitable sleeping rooms, with the paperwork to prove it.

Is a garage bedroom cold?

A converted one shouldn't be, and a compliant one can't be: the insulation standards for the floor build-up, wall linings and ceiling are the same as for a new house. The cold-garage-bedroom stories come from conversions done without building control involvement.

What does the en-suite add to the timeline?

Usually one to two weeks — drainage connection, tanking, tiling and the extract system. If your garage is on the opposite side of the house from the drains, we'll price the run honestly at survey rather than surprising you later.

Do covenants apply to bedrooms too?

Covenants restrict what happens to the garage regardless of what the new room is used for — a bedroom conversion on a covenanted new-build estate needs the same developer-consent route as any other. It's one of the three checks in every survey we do.
